MCN Test Prep

Support and contact

MCN Test Prep is a study app for FAA knowledge test question banks. It stores your questions and your progress on your device, and it works without an internet connection.

Getting started

Loading your questions

The app reads question banks as JSON files. Put the file in iCloud Drive, AirDrop it to your phone, or save it to Files. Then open the app and use the menu in the top corner of the Progress screen to load it.

The app holds two banks at once, each with its own progress, missed list and test history. Switch between them with the control at the top of the Progress screen, or from the options menu while studying.

Study modes

Answer only shows each question with just the correct answer, for reading through material quickly. Multiple choice marks your answer right or wrong straight away. Swipe left or right to move between questions, or use the Previous and Next buttons.

Practice tests

Build a random test of 10, 25, 50, 100 or all questions, drawn from everything, from questions you haven't gotten right yet, or from your missed list. Nothing is marked until you finish. The pass mark shown is 70%.

Missed questions

Anything you get wrong in Study or in a test is collected on the Missed tab. Review them as multiple choice; answering one correctly clears it from the list. Swipe left on a question to remove it yourself.

Common questions

The app says a question has no marked answer

Your JSON file has no correct answer recorded for that question. It still appears in Answer only mode, but it's left out of multiple choice and practice tests. Check the source file if you expected an answer there.

A question refers to a figure I can't see

Some FAA questions refer to figures in the official testing supplement, which the app doesn't include. Those questions need the printed or PDF supplement alongside the app.

My progress disappeared

Progress is tied to each question's ID. If you load a bank whose IDs differ from the one you were studying, the app can't match your old progress to the new questions. Loading the same file again restores it.

Can I start over?

Yes. On the Progress screen, open the menu in the top corner and choose to reset progress. It clears only the bank you're currently viewing; the other one is untouched.

Is my data backed up?

Everything lives on your device and is included in your normal device backups. Deleting the app deletes your questions and progress with it.
