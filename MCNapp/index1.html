MCN Test App

Support and contact

MCN Test App is a study tool for the FAA Military Competence — Non-Category (MCN) knowledge test. It covers pilot privileges and limitations, air traffic and airspace, and accident reporting under 49 CFR 830. Study in two modes, take practice tests, and review the questions you miss. Everything runs on your iPhone with no account and no internet connection.

Using the app

Two ways to study

Answer only shows each question with just the correct answer, for reading through material quickly. Multiple choice marks your answer right or wrong straight away and keeps score. Swipe left or right to move between questions, or use the Previous and Next buttons.

The options menu lets you shuffle, narrow the set to questions you haven't studied or haven't gotten right yet, and jump to a question number.

Practice tests

Build a test of 10, 25, 50, 100 or all questions, drawn from everything, from the ones you haven't gotten right yet, or from your missed list. Nothing is marked until you finish. Results show your percentage against a 70% pass mark and list every question you got wrong.

Missed questions

Anything you get wrong in study or in a test collects on the Missed tab. Review them as multiple choice; answering one correctly clears it from the list. Swipe left on a question to remove it yourself.

Two question banks

The app carries two banks, each with its own progress, missed list and test history. Switch between them with the control at the top of the Progress screen, or from the options menu while studying. You can also load your own question file in JSON format from the menu in the top corner of the Progress screen.

Common questions

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Everything works offline, and your progress is saved on the device.

Is my progress backed up?

Your progress is stored on your iPhone and is included in your normal iCloud or computer backups. Deleting the app deletes its data with it.

How do I start over?

On the Progress screen, open the menu in the top corner and choose to reset progress. It clears only the bank you're viewing; the other one is untouched.

A question mentions a figure I can't see

Some FAA questions refer to figures in the official Airman Knowledge Testing Supplement, which isn't part of this app. Keep the supplement, which the FAA publishes free at faa.gov, alongside the app for those questions.

I think an answer is wrong

Please email me the question number and what you believe the correct answer is. Regulations change, and corrections go into the next update.

Does this replace the official test?

No. It's a study aid. The real MCN test is 50 questions in 2 hours with a 70% pass mark, taken at an FAA-authorized testing center.
