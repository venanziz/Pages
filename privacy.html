Privacy Policy

MCN Test Prep · last updated 22 September 2026

The short version: MCN Test Prep collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. Your questions and your progress stay on your device.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There is no sign-up, no login, and no user profile. I have no way to see what you study, how you score, or that you use the app at all.

Information stored on your device

Everything the app needs is kept in its own storage on your iPhone:

  • Question banks you load into the app, saved as files inside the app's container.
  • Study progress: which questions you've viewed, your right and wrong answers, your missed list, and your practice test scores.
  • Settings, such as which bank you last had selected and the name of the file you loaded.

This data never leaves your device unless you send it somewhere yourself. It is included in your iCloud or computer backups the same way other app data is, under Apple's terms rather than mine. Deleting the app removes all of it permanently.

Files you import

When you load a question bank, iOS shows you the system file picker and the app reads only the file you choose. It has no access to the rest of your files, and the file you pick is copied into the app's own storage. Nothing is uploaded.

Network access

The app does not connect to the internet. It has no analytics or crash-reporting frameworks, no advertising code, and no third-party trackers of any kind. No third party receives data from this app, because the app sends none.

Crash reports and App Store data

If you have opted in to sharing analytics with developers in your iOS Settings, Apple may provide me with aggregate, anonymized crash and usage statistics. That data comes from Apple, is not tied to your identity, and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. You can turn it off under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

The app is a study tool for aviation knowledge tests and isn't directed at children. Since it collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your choices

You control everything the app stores. You can reset the progress for a bank from the menu on the Progress screen, replace a bank by loading a different file, or delete the app to remove all of its data at once. There is nothing for me to delete on your behalf, because I hold nothing.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. If a future version were to collect anything, it would say so plainly here and ask you first.
